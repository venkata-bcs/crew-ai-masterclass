## Overview
The United States of America (USA), also known as the United States (U.S.) or America, is a country primarily located in North America. It is a federal republic consisting of 50 states and a federal capital district, Washington, D.C. The 48 contiguous states border Canada to the north and Mexico to the south, with the semi-exclave of Alaska in the northwest and the archipelago of Hawaii in the Pacific Ocean.

The United States also asserts sovereignty over five major island territories and various uninhabited islands in Oceania and the Caribbean. It is a megadiverse country, with the world's fourth-largest total area and third-largest population, exceeding 341 million.

## History
- Paleo-Indians migrated from North Asia to North America at least 15,000 years ago.
- British colonization began with the establishment of the Thirteen Colonies starting in 1607.
- Independence declared July 2, 1776 (Lee Resolution); Revolutionary War concluded with victory in 1783.
- Civil War (1861–1865) over slavery; Union victory led to abolition of slavery.
- U.S. emerged as a great power by 1900 after involvement in World War I.
- Entered World War II following the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor in 1941.
- Cold War rivalry with the Soviet Union lasted until the Soviet collapse in 1991.
- Remained the world's sole superpower, facing increasing competition from China in the 2020s.

## Geography & Climate
- Located primarily in North America, spanning from the Atlantic to the Pacific Ocean.
- Consists of 48 contiguous states between Canada and Mexico, plus Alaska (northwest) and Hawaii (Pacific archipelago).
- Includes five major inhabited territories and numerous uninhabited islands in the Caribbean and Pacific.
- Megadiverse with a wide variety of ecosystems and wildlife.
- Fourth-largest country by total area (approximately 9.83 million km²).

## Economy
- Largest economy in the world by nominal GDP, accounting for over a quarter of global output.
- Highest disposable household income per capita among OECD member countries.
- Significant wealth inequality despite overall high income.
- Responsible for nearly one-third of global military spending.
- Leading member of international organizations such as the UN, NATO, World Bank, and IMF.
