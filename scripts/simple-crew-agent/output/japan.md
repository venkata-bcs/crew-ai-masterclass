## Overview
Japan is an island country in East Asia. Located in the Pacific Ocean off the northeast coast of the Asian mainland, it is bordered to the west by the Sea of Japan, the Sea of Okhotsk in the north, and the East China Sea in the south. The Japanese archipelago consists of four major islands alongside over 14,000 smaller islands.

Japan is divided into 47 administrative prefectures and eight traditional regions, and around 75% of its terrain is mountainous and heavily forested, concentrating its agriculture and highly urbanized population along its eastern coastal plains. With a population of almost 123 million as of 2026, it is the world's 11th most populous country. Tokyo is the country's capital and largest city.

## History
- c. 36,000 BC: Beginning of the Japanese Paleolithic, first known habitation.
- ~14,500 BC: Start of the Jōmon period, characterized by pit dwelling and rudimentary agriculture.
- 660 BC: Legendary founding of Japan by Emperor Jimmu.
- 552 AD: Buddhism introduced from Baekje (Korea).
- 645 AD: Taika Reforms enacted to centralize government.
- 794 AD: Capital moved to Heian-kyō (Kyoto), beginning of the Heian period.
- 1185: Minamoto clan defeats Taira clan, establishing samurai-led feudal era.
- 1600: Tokugawa shogunate unifies Japan, initiating isolationist foreign policy.
- 1868: Meiji Restoration restores imperial power.
- 1945: Japan surrenders after atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
- Early 1990s: Collapse of the asset price bubble leads to the Lost Decades.

## Geography & Climate
- Japan is an island country in East Asia located in the Pacific Ocean.
- It is bordered by the Sea of Japan to the west, the Sea of Okhotsk to the north, and the East China Sea to the south.
- The Japanese archipelago consists of four major islands and over 14,000 smaller islands.
- Around 75% of Japan's terrain is mountainous and heavily forested.
- Agriculture and population are concentrated along the eastern coastal plains.

## Economy
- Japan is a developed country with one of the world's largest economies by nominal GDP.
- It is a global leader in the automotive, electronics, and robotics industries.
- Japan makes significant contributions to science and technology.
- After World War II, Japan experienced rapid economic growth.
- Since the early 1990s, Japan has undergone economic stagnation known as the Lost Decades.

## Culture
- Japanese popular culture includes animation (anime) and comics (manga).
- Japanese cuisine, such as sushi and ramen, is renowned worldwide.
- Japan leads in video game development and technology.
- Japanese cinema and television have significant global influence.
- Traditional elements like festivals, tea ceremony, and kimono are integral to Japanese culture.

## Fun Facts
- Japan consists of over 14,000 islands.
- About three-quarters of Japan's land area is mountainous and forested.
- Japan has one of the world's highest life expectancies.
